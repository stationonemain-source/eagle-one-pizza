The short version

The pizza is ahead of the website.

Eagle One has 6,027 Google reviews across seven stores, ratings from 4.3 to 4.6, and the first map result for “pizza” in six of its seven towns. The website still has its 2012 design. The menu is a picture, a phone shows it at desktop size, and the newest photo on it was taken in June 2012.

The bigger problem is the one behind every “Order Online” button. It runs on GloriaFood, and GloriaFood shuts down on 30 April 2027. We have already built the replacement site. It’s live below as a private preview. What’s left is to move ordering to a system that isn’t closing, put the real menu and photos in, and switch eagleonepizza.com over.

The deadline

Online ordering switches off in April

213days to 30 April 2027

GloriaFood’s own homepage says the service “will reach end of life on April 30, 2027”, and Oracle, which owns it, offers no direct replacement. On that day all fourteen “Order Online” buttons on your site stop working, at every store.

The menus, customer list and order history inside it go with it unless they are exported first. Moving in the slow months is easy. Moving in April, with every other GloriaFood restaurant trying to do the same, is not.

Good news

A reputation most chains would pay for

6,027 Google reviews, and Google’s first map result for “pizza” in Midwest City, Harrah, Norman, north OKC, Lexington and Holdenville. The site is also clean: no malware, no planted spam links, and names, addresses and phones match Google at every store.

Ordering

The ordering system closes on 30 April 2027

All fourteen Order Online buttons open GloriaFood. Nothing replaces it automatically, and the menus and customer list inside it go when it does.

Menu

The menu is a picture, and the prices disagree

Every price is inside two flyer images. A large one-topping pizza is $6.99 in one, $10.99 in the other and $8.99 in the ordering menu. The ordering page shows a 10% pick-up deal right above a 35% pick-up deal.

Phones

A shrunken desktop page on every phone

No page has a mobile layout, so body text lands at about 5 pixels tall. “Call us” on the homepage opens the ordering page instead of the phone. Speed on mobile scores 57 of 100.

Google

Seven listings, one homepage

Every listing links the same homepage, six over the old http address. Lexington is filed as “Restaurant”, not pizza. The Norman listing carries a review about a nail salon. The 2023 Readers’ Choice award appears only inside a flyer image.

Tracking

Nothing counted since 2023

The only analytics tag is Universal Analytics, which Google switched off in July 2023. There is no email or text list, so online customers can’t be invited back.

The plan

1. Finish the website

  • A page for each of the seven stores, with its hours, phone, delivery area and photo
  • The full menu in text, one list for all stores, with any store-by-store price differences
  • A new photo shoot to replace the restored 2012 photos, if you want one
  • Your approval on every word, price and deal

2. Move online ordering

  • Export menus, customers and order history from GloriaFood for all seven stores
  • Set up ordering on a system that isn’t closing, chosen with you before any work starts
  • Menus, deals, delivery zones and store hours rebuilt and tested with a real order at each store
  • Every button switched over well before 30 April 2027

3. Get found

  • Seven Google listings: each linked to its own store page over https, Lexington filed as a pizza restaurant, fresh photos, the nail-salon review reported
  • Restaurant details and menu marked up for Google and AI assistants
  • Google Analytics 4, so you can see how many orders start on the site

4. Launch on eagleonepizza.com

  • Move the site onto your domain with a secure connection
  • Old page addresses forward to the right new pages, so no links break
  • The Weebly site retired once the new one is confirmed live
